Your cycle

Because your hormones decide
what kind of day your skin gets.

Week 1 · Menstrual week

Your period week

dulls everything down. Skin feels dry, sensitive and a little tired of being touched.

Week 2 · Follicular week

And then, the glow.

Oestrogen rises and skin bounces back — the week it just needs to be kept clean, not stripped.

Peak week.

Skin is at its brightest — but a little more oil creeps in by the evening.

Week 4 · Luteal week
